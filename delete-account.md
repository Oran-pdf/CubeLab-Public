# Delete Your CubeLab Account

CubeLab allows you to permanently delete your account and associated data.

## Delete Your Account in CubeLab

If you still have access to the CubeLab app:

1. Open **CubeLab**.
2. Go to **Settings**.
3. Select **Delete Account**.
4. Follow the instructions to confirm deletion.

Deleting the CubeLab app from your device does **not** delete your account. Your account and saved progress remain available so that you can reinstall CubeLab and sign in again later.

## If You No Longer Have Access to the App

If you cannot access CubeLab and would like your account deleted, send a deletion request to:

**[cubelab.contact.app@gmail.com](mailto:cubelab.contact.app@gmail.com)**

Please send the request from the email address associated with your CubeLab account so that we can verify that the account belongs to you.

## What Is Deleted

When your CubeLab account is deleted, data associated with your account will be permanently deleted or disassociated from you, including:

* Your email address
* Your display name
* Your CubeLab account identifier
* Your saved progress and numerical statistics
* Your leaderboard score and ranking

Certain information may be retained where necessary for legitimate legal, security, fraud-prevention, or financial-record-keeping requirements.

Account deletion cannot be undone.
