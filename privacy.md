# Privacy Policy for CubeLab

**Last updated: September 7, 2026**

CubeLab respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected when you use the CubeLab app, how that information is used, and the choices available to you.

## Information We Collect

When you create and use a CubeLab account, we may collect and store:

* Your email address
* Your chosen display name
* A unique account identifier
* Numerical statistics relating to your progress and activity within CubeLab
* Your leaderboard score and ranking

Account authentication and associated data are managed using Supabase.

## How We Use Your Information

We use this information to:

* Create and maintain your CubeLab account
* Allow you to sign in and restore your account and progress
* Save and synchronise your progress and statistics
* Provide the CubeLab leaderboard
* Operate and maintain the app
* Manage access to free and subscription features

CubeLab does not use your personal information for advertising and does not sell your personal information.

## Leaderboard

If you participate in the CubeLab leaderboard, your **display name, score, and ranking** may be visible to other CubeLab users.

Your email address and other private account information are not displayed on the leaderboard.

## Third-Party Services

CubeLab uses **Supabase** to provide account authentication and cloud data storage. Information required to provide these services may therefore be processed by Supabase in accordance with its own privacy practices.

CubeLab may also use services provided by the relevant app store to process subscriptions and purchases. Payment information is handled by the app store/payment provider and is not stored by CubeLab.

## Data Retention

Your account information and app progress may be retained for as long as your CubeLab account remains active.

Uninstalling CubeLab does not delete your CubeLab account or the information associated with it. This allows you to reinstall the app and regain access to your saved progress by signing in again.

You can permanently delete your account from within CubeLab.

## Account and Data Deletion

You can delete your CubeLab account from within the app by going to **Settings → Delete Account**.

Deleting your account will permanently delete or disassociate the personal data associated with your CubeLab account, subject to any information that must be retained for legitimate legal, security, fraud-prevention, or financial-record-keeping purposes.

For more information about account deletion, please see the CubeLab Account Deletion page.

## Children's Privacy

CubeLab is intended for a general audience and is not specifically directed at children.

## Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information associated with CubeLab accounts. Authentication and cloud data storage are provided using Supabase.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission over the internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. If it is changed, the updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
